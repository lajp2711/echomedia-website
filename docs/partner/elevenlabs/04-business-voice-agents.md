## Business Problem

Most businesses leak time through repeated doorway conversations:

- What services do you offer?
- How much does it cost?
- How do I book?
- What happens next?
- Can someone call me back?
- Where do I upload information?
- What do I need before the appointment?

A well-designed voice agent can answer, route, collect, qualify, and escalate.

## Demo Concept: Support Intake Voice Agent

Demo name: EchoSupport Intake Voice Agent

Use case:

A customer or employee speaks with a voice assistant that collects support issue details and prepares a structured ticket for human review.

Intake fields:

- Name
- Company/team
- Contact information
- Affected system
- Issue description
- When it started
- Number of users affected
- Error message
- Screenshots/log availability
- Business impact
- Urgency
- Preferred follow-up

OpsHelm connection:

Voice intake -> structured ticket -> AI analysis -> internal summary -> customer response draft.

## Demo Concept: Campaign/Constituent Intake Voice Agent

Demo name: Lantern Civic Intake Voice Agent

Use case:

A citizen or supporter speaks with a voice assistant that collects their concern, categorizes the issue, captures location and contact preference, and routes the item for human review.

Issue categories:

- Veterans affairs
- Healthcare
- Housing
- Public safety
- Economy/jobs
- Education
- Infrastructure
- Civil liberties
- Local government
- Other

Safety note:

This workflow must include clear disclaimers, human review, privacy boundaries, and emergency redirection.
