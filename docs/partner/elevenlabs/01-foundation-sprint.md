## PartnerStack Setup Checklist

1. Sign into the ElevenLabs affiliate / PartnerStack dashboard.
2. Confirm the account email.
3. Open the ElevenLabs partner program.
4. Copy the primary affiliate link.
5. Create campaign-specific custom links.
6. Set up withdrawal method under Commissions -> Setup Withdrawals.
7. Confirm dashboard visibility for clicks, signups, revenue, referrals, and commissions.
8. Save all links in the tracking sheet.
9. Add affiliate disclosure to each page containing affiliate links.
10. Test click each campaign link and confirm tracking.
