# ElevenLabs Partner Launch Overview

## Executive Goal

Launch the ElevenLabs affiliate partnership as a practical revenue and services funnel for EchoMedia.ai under the Lantern Protocol umbrella.

This is not just an affiliate link. It is a voice-commerce layer for authors, creators, businesses, short-term rental operators, support teams, and civic/campaign workflows.

## Brand Architecture

- **Lantern Protocol**: parent mythos, governance world, and strategic brand universe.
- **EchoMedia.ai**: AI automation and media studio.
- **EchoChamber**: voice, language, translation, and pronunciation layer.
- **ElevenLabs**: recommended AI audio platform used in workflows where appropriate.

## Public Positioning

> EchoMedia.ai helps authors, creators, and businesses turn written content into narrated, translated, dubbed, and interactive voice experiences using AI audio tools like ElevenLabs.

## Risk Controls

- Use affiliate disclosure wherever affiliate links appear.
- Only clone voices with explicit permission.
- Add AI disclosure where appropriate.
- Keep human review for sensitive domains.
- Clearly separate fiction, demos, and real-world claims.
- Add emergency redirection for civic, medical, housing, legal, or safety-adjacent workflows.
