## The Problem

Most manuscripts are written for the eye, not the ear.

Common issues:

- Chapter headings are inconsistent
- Preface or manifest content gets included accidentally
- Tables, links, and notes read awkwardly
- Page numbers or headers sneak into narration
- Dialogue pacing sounds wrong
- Pronunciation problems are discovered too late
- Regenerating every chapter becomes time-consuming

The fix: prepare one clean, audio-ready manuscript before generating the full book.

## EchoMedia.ai Author Workflow

2. Format the book for audio.
3. Generate a test chapter.
4. Revise the source manuscript.
5. Listen like an editor.
6. Publish strategically.
