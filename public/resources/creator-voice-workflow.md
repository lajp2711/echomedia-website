# Creator Voice Workflow

A build-in-public workflow for creator AI voice systems.

## The Problem

Creators need more content than they have hours. Voice content is powerful, but it also carries trust. A sloppy AI voice workflow can sound efficient while quietly damaging the brand.

The answer is not to generate everything. The answer is to create a small, repeatable system where the creator keeps control.

## The Workflow

1. Define the voice owner and approved use cases.
2. Capture consent and boundaries.
3. Build reusable scripts and prompt templates.
4. Generate short samples first.
5. Review tone, accuracy, and platform fit.
6. Approve only the content that sounds aligned.
7. Publish with disclosure where appropriate.
8. Track performance and audience response.

## Example Weekly System

- Monday: 30-second voiceover for short-form post.
- Wednesday: educational clip from a longer article or script.
- Friday: offer or lead magnet voiceover.
- Monthly: review what worked, update prompts, retire weak formats.
